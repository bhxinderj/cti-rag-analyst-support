\section*{Rubric dimension definitions}

The analyst rubric of Section~\ref{sec:ragas-metrics} scores every
answer on four dimensions with an integer 0--3 scale.
Table~\ref{tab:rubric-definitions} states the definition of each
dimension together with its lowest and highest score anchor. The
scoring instruction given to the judge model contains the full
four-step scale and is part of the evaluation module in the
repository.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \small
    \begin{tabular}{lp{0.26\textwidth}p{0.24\textwidth}p{0.26\textwidth}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Dimension} & \textbf{Judges} & \textbf{Score 0} &
        \textbf{Score 3} \\
        \midrule
        Prioritization & severity signal visibility and rationale &
            no severity signal, or severity contradicts the ground
            truth &
            severity prominent at the top with a rationale tied to
            concrete signals (CVSS, KEV, ransomware use) \\
        Actionability & defensive guidance quality &
            no mitigations, or only generic advice &
            discrete, source-grounded mitigations an analyst could
            hand to an operator \\
        Completeness & template-field coverage &
            major fields silently missing &
            all expected fields present, or missing fields explicitly
            flagged as gaps \\
        Traceability & citation quality &
            no citations, or citations without a retrieved source &
            every factual claim cited with a correct source label \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
    \caption{Rubric dimensions with their lowest and highest score
             anchors (0--3 integer scale)}
    \label{tab:rubric-definitions}
\end{table}
