\section*{Judge configuration}

All RAGAS and rubric scores of the final series were produced by the
judge model \texttt{gpt-4o-mini} (accessed via OpenRouter), configured
identically across all configurations and runs. The RAGAS input
follows the rule of Section~\ref{sec:ragas-metrics}: rated is the
Layer-2 narrative without the gaps section
(\texttt{ragas\_answer\_field: l2\_output\_sans\_gaps} in the run
metadata). The rubric judge re-rates the stored answers of each run
against the dimension definitions of
Appendix~\ref{app:query-set}. Every run artifact records the judge
model, the snapshot date (2025-12-31), and the per-sample scores. The
artifacts are versioned in the repository
(Appendix~\ref{app:repository}).

\section*{Per-query results of the primary configuration}

Table~\ref{tab:per-query-v4} reports the per-query RAGAS scores of the
templated hybrid pipeline (V4) as three-run means, complementing the
aggregate view of Table~\ref{tab:generation-results}. The spread
across queries is wide: on average, cross-source queries reach the
highest answer correctness (0.65) and vulnerability queries the
highest context recall (0.75), while the open TTP-correlation queries
mark the lower end (correctness 0.34). The aggregate means of
Chapter~\ref{ch:results} average over this heterogeneity.
